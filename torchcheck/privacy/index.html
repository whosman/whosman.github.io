Privacy Policy

TorchCheck · Effective May 6, 2026

TorchCheck verifies flashlight and headlamp brightness claims using your phone's camera. This page explains what the app does with your data. The short version: nothing leaves your phone.

Nothing leaves your device. TorchCheck does not transmit, upload, or share data with us or any third party. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the app uses

The app uses your phone's camera to measure light output. Camera frames are analyzed locally in real time. They are not saved as images, not uploaded, and not retained after a test ends.

The app reads your phone's battery level during a test so it can record how much battery the test consumed. This stays on your device.

The app saves your test results — brightness measurements over time, the device tested, the mode, your notes — in a database stored only on your phone.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, email, location, or contacts
  • Photos, microphone, or files outside the app's own storage
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or telemetry events
  • Any sensor not listed above

Sharing your results — only if you opt in

A future version of TorchCheck may include an opt-in feature to contribute test results to a public dataset that helps others compare flashlights. If you choose to opt in, only the test data — brightness curves, model name, mode — would be shared, never personal information. This feature does not currently exist in the app, and nothing will be shared without an explicit opt-in choice on your part.

Children

TorchCheck is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be communicated in-app before they take effect.

Contact

Questions or concerns: support@torchcheck.com
